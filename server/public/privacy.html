Privacy Policy

Otaku Sync · Last updated: 26 August 2026

This policy explains what the Otaku Sync browser extension collects, why, and what we do not collect. Plain language, no surprises.

What we collect

  • Your phone number. Used only to sign you in and to identify your subscription. Stored on our server.
  • Account status. When your trial started, whether a subscription is active, and when it renews.
  • Party codes. The six-digit code of a party while it is running, so two people can be connected. Not stored after it ends.

What we do not collect

  • We do not store your chat messages, reactions or GIFs. They pass through our relay to the other person and are never written to disk.
  • We do not record, store or view your webcam. Camera video travels directly between the two browsers and never reaches our servers.
  • We do not collect your Crunchyroll credentials — the extension never sees or touches them.
  • We do not track your browsing history, and we do not build advertising profiles. There are no third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We do not store card or payment details. Payments are handled entirely by Razorpay.

Which titles you watch

To keep two players in step, the extension sends the episode identifier and playback position to the other person in your party, through our relay. We do not log or retain this — it exists only in transit.

Third parties

  • Razorpay processes payments and receives what it needs for that. See Razorpay's own privacy policy.
  • An SMS provider delivers your one-time login code and receives your phone number for that purpose.
  • GIPHY serves GIF search results when you open the GIF picker. Your search terms are sent to GIPHY.
  • STUN servers help two browsers find each other for webcam connections. They see network addresses, not video.

How long we keep things

Account records are kept while your account exists. One-time codes expire after five minutes. Ask us to delete your account and we remove your phone number and account record within 30 days.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS or secure WebSockets. Login codes are stored hashed, never in plain text. Login tokens live in your browser's extension storage.

Children

Otaku Sync is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by messaging support. Uninstalling the extension stops all collection.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above changes and the extension will show a notice.
