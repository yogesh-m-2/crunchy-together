Refund & Cancellation Policy

Otaku Sync · Last updated: 26 August 2026

Try before you pay

Every account gets 7 days free. Use that period to check the extension works with your setup before any money changes hands.

Cancelling

You can cancel your subscription at any time from inside the extension or by messaging support on WhatsApp. Cancellation stops future charges. You keep access until the end of the cycle you have already paid for.

Refunds

  • Something broken on our side: if the service was unusable for a significant part of a billing cycle and we could not fix it, message us and we will refund that cycle in full.
  • Charged after cancelling: refunded in full, always.
  • Accidental or duplicate charge: refunded in full.
  • Changed your mind within 7 days of a charge: message us and we will refund that cycle.

Because the amount is small and the trial is generous, we would rather refund you than argue about it.

What is not refunded

Cycles already fully used beyond the window above, and situations outside our control — for example your own streaming subscription lapsing, or your network blocking the connection.

How to request one

Message +91 86600 95124 on WhatsApp with the phone number on your account and roughly when you were charged. We reply within 2 business days.

Timing

Approved refunds are issued through Razorpay to the original payment method. Banks typically take 5–7 business days to show the credit.
