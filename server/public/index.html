Chrome extension

Otaku Sync

Watch anime with a friend, actually together. One person starts a party, shares the link, and both players stay in step — pause, play and skip apply to both of you at once.

What you get

  • Synced play, pause and seek, with drift correction
  • Chat, emoji, GIFs and floating reactions
  • Optional webcam windows, peer-to-peer
  • Auto-follow when an episode ends and the next one starts

Pricing

7 days free. After that ₹5 per month. Cancel any time.

Requirements

Google Chrome, and each viewer needs their own Crunchyroll account. This extension only synchronises playback between two browsers — it does not stream, host, download or redistribute any video.
